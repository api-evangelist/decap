Decap Turbo is a cloud-based upgrade for teams already using Decap CMS in production. It is built to remove the operational overhead around auth and permissions, while making the CMS experience noticeably faster for editors. Decap CMS stays open source Decap CMS remains the same open source, self-installed Git-based CMS the community relies on.
